# WI-099 — Eye and Ping expire on a visible countdown; the fog reveal is not stranded

**Spec:** SPEC-046 §1 · **From:** IN-086 · **Model:** `sonnet` · **Effort:** S · **Closed:**
2026-09-03

The ping already self-expired from RTDB at a fixed 3s but drew a constant ring for the
whole window, so a player who looked up a second late saw nothing and couldn't tell
whether they'd missed a ping. The eye never expired at all — a referee who checked a
sightline and moved on left a translucent visibility polygon and a dot on the map for the
rest of the session. Both now carry a lifetime that is legible from the mark itself, and
the eye's countdown is gated so it never clears the "reveal what the eye can see" action
out from under a referee who is still deciding whether to press it.

## Changes made

- **`packages/shared/src/store/campaign-store.ts`** — new exported `PING_TTL_MS = 3000`
  next to `PingPos`, replacing what were three independent copies of the literal `3000`
  with one source of truth.
- **`packages/shared/src/store/memory-store.ts`**, **`firebase-store.ts`** — each drops its
  own local `PING_TTL_MS` const and imports the shared one instead. The removal timer's
  behavior is unchanged (still 3000ms, still enforced in both store implementations, per
  SPEC-046 §1 rule 2).
- **`apps/web/src/lib/map/vector-engine.ts`**
  - `renderPings` now keeps `{ node, ts }` per ping (the RTDB doc's own timestamp) instead
    of a bare `PIXI.Graphics`, and redraws each ring's radius (14 → 5px) and stroke alpha
    (1 → 0) as a function of `(Date.now() - ts) / PING_TTL_MS`. A `tickPings` function
    re-draws every live ping each frame, attached to `app.ticker` only while at least one
    ping is live and removed once none are — the same on/off pattern the existing
    grid-redraw ticker uses, so idle maps don't pay a per-frame cost for a rare event.
    `destroy()` removes the ticker too.
  - `ToolPreviewInput` gains `eyeAlpha: number` (1 = just placed, 0 = about to clear). The
    eye's dot (`renderToolPreview`) now fades from full opacity down to a 0.25 floor
    (never fully invisible while `eye` itself is still set, so the dot doesn't disappear
    ahead of the visibility polygon it belongs to and read as a rendering bug).
- **`apps/web/src/lib/components/VectorMapView.svelte`**
  - New client-local state: `EYE_LIFETIME_MS` (4000), `eyeRemainingMs`, and the derived
    `eyeAlpha = eyeRemainingMs / EYE_LIFETIME_MS` passed into `renderToolPreview`.
  - `startEyeTimer()` — a 100ms `setInterval` that decrements `eyeRemainingMs` and clears
    `eye` at zero, **except while `mapCtrl.canRevealFromEye` is true**: the clock simply
    doesn't advance then (SPEC-046 §1 rule 4), rather than being restarted or extended, so
    it resumes with whatever life was left the moment the referee acts or moves on.
  - The `tool === 'eye'` click branch resets `eyeRemainingMs` and (re)starts the timer each
    time a new eye is placed.
  - `onDestroy` clears the interval alongside the other teardown.

## Visible behavior changes

- **A ping now visibly counts down**: its ring shrinks and fades over the same 3 seconds it
  already lived for, instead of holding constant and vanishing without notice.
- **A placed eye now clears itself** after about 4 seconds of being unused, fading its dot
  as it goes — it no longer sits on the map for the rest of the session.
- **The eye's countdown pauses while "reveal from here" is available** (fog on, Eye tool
  still active, eye placed): the mark and its LoS preview stay put until the referee
  presses Reveal or switches away, so the button can't disappear mid-decision.
- Nothing about placement changed: both tools still take a plain click at the raw pointer
  position, and no `data-testid` moved.

## How to verify

- `pnpm verify` — lint + typecheck + unit, all green.
- By eye: open a map, place a Ping and watch its ring shrink/fade over ~3s instead of
  cutting out abruptly. Switch to Eye with fog **off**, click to place one, and watch its
  dot fade out and clear after ~4s. Turn fog **on**, place an eye, and confirm the dot
  (and the Reveal-from-eye action) stays available indefinitely while the Eye tool stays
  active; switch to another tool or turn fog off and confirm the countdown resumes and the
  eye eventually clears.

## Deviations

- **Exact lifetimes are new numbers, not cited by the spec.** SPEC-046 §1 fixes the ping's
  lifetime at the existing 3s but leaves "the eye gets a lifetime of its own" unspecified;
  4000ms was chosen as a comparable, referee-legible window and is not load-bearing on any
  other behavior.
- **Consolidating `PING_TTL_MS` into one shared export** was not explicitly called for by
  §1's numbered rules, but rule 2 anticipates it ("If the constant does move it moves in
  both implementations together") and the renderer's countdown needed the same value the
  stores enforce, so duplicating it a third time in `vector-engine.ts` would have created
  exactly the drift rule 2 is warning against. Logged here rather than as a separate
  intake item since it was required to keep the countdown honest against the real TTL.

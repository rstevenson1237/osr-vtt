# WI-120 — Land the Worldographer terrain art pack

**Closed 2026-09-09.** From IN-114, against SPEC-047 §8 (DEC-088, DEC-089). Model target
`sonnet`, run on `sonnet`. Takes WI-119's approved reference sheet
(`docs/completed/wi-119/palette.json`, `docs/completed/WI-119.md`) as input.

§8 replaces WI-101's terrain art with the public-domain Worldographer/Inkwell Ideas B&W and
multicoloured-classic icon sets, on the terms DEC-088 settled, with a new colour model DEC-089
answered: an authored `color`/`ink` pair per kind instead of `hexOverlayTone`'s two-grey
threshold. This item lands WI-119's proposal — the traced files, the catalog rewrite, the
`ATTRIBUTION.md` entry, and the SPEC-030 §2 annotation.

## Changes made

- **`apps/web/public/assets/hex/terrain/*.svg`** — 41 traced files added or replaced. 34 are
  wholly new kinds; 6 (`desert`, `forest`, `hills`, `jungle`, `mountains`, `swamp`) supersede
  an existing kind's file in place; `grassland` is a new file traced from the multicoloured
  set's `grassyhills.png` (not the B&W set's own sparser `bw-grassland.png`). 13 files from
  WI-101's pack are removed: the 6 superseded kinds, the 6 kinds that now alias another kind's
  art (`grass`, `ice-floe`, `palm`, `plateau`, `scrub`, `tundra`), and `water` (now
  background-only). The 7 kinds this pack has no equivalent shape for (`barren`,
  `mountain-major`, `mountain-minor`, `plains`, `reed`, `tree-deciduous`, `tree-evergreen`)
  keep their WI-101 art untouched.
- **`packages/shared/src/map/hex/catalog.ts`** — `HEX_TERRAIN_CATALOG` rewritten to 56 rows:
  the 41 authored kinds (`water` among them, background-only), the 6 aliases (unchanged
  `color`, `ref`/`ink` borrowed from the kind whose art they now share), the 8 untouched kinds
  (unchanged `color`/`ref`, a baked-in `ink`), and `unknown`. `HexTerrainEntry` gains `ink:
  string | null` and `ref` becomes `string | null` (both `null` together for `water`, the one
  kind with no overlay). `hexOverlayTone`, `HEX_OVERLAY_DARK` and `HEX_OVERLAY_LIGHT` are
  retired. New exports: `contrastRatio`, `blendHexColors`, `HEX_TERRAIN_CONTRAST_MIN` (3.3),
  `HEX_TERRAIN_OVERLAY_ALPHA` (moved here from `vector-engine.ts`, unchanged at 0.55).
  `colorLuminance` is refactored onto a shared `parseHexColor` helper; its own behaviour
  (including its mid-grey fallback for unparseable input) is unchanged.
- **`packages/shared/src/map/hex/catalog.test.ts`** — rewritten: the `hexOverlayTone` describe
  block is gone with the function; new coverage for `colorLuminance`/`contrastRatio`/
  `blendHexColors`, an alias-resolution check, a `volcano`-in-both-catalogs check, and the
  contrast guard itself (both the raw pair and the 55%-alpha-blended-vs-contents figure),
  scoped to the 40 authored-with-overlay kinds (see Deviations #1).
- **`apps/web/src/lib/map/vector-engine.ts`** — `renderHexTiles` reads `entry.ink` directly as
  the overlay tint instead of `hexMap.hexOverlayTone(entry.color)`, and skips the terrain-art
  placement entirely when `ref`/`ink` are `null` (the fill still paints — only the overlay is
  skipped).
- **`apps/web/src/lib/components/shell/HexTilePanel.svelte`** — the terrain swatch's preview
  moves from an `<img>` with a binary `brightness(0)` CSS filter (driven by the retired
  `overlayDark`/`hexOverlayTone`) to a `mask-image` `<span>` whose `background-color` is
  `entry.ink` — the CSS equivalent of a white-authored-art-plus-multiply-tint for an arbitrary
  hue, not just two greys. Renders nothing for a kind with no `ref`/`ink` (`water`).
- **`apps/web/src/lib/components/MapToolbar.svelte`** — `hexTerrainPreview` returns `null`
  instead of resolving a `ref` when the selected terrain kind is `water`, matching the existing
  `doorPreview` pattern; `previewFor`'s callers already handle a `null` preview.
- **`apps/web/public/assets/ATTRIBUTION.md`** — the `hex/terrain/*.svg` paragraph is split from
  the contents/unknown paragraph (which is now WI-101-and-earlier, unchanged) into its own
  section recording the Worldographer/Inkwell Ideas provenance, the trace method, the
  public-domain licence statement's sourcing (project owner's confirmation plus a web-search
  summary — not fetched verbatim, both `worldographer.com` and `hexographer.com` being blocked
  by this project's egress proxy), and which kinds now alias another kind's art.
- **`docs/spec/SPEC-030.md`** §2 — a `WI-120` amendment blockquote added, retiring "drawn in a
  contrasting light/dark tone" in favour of the authored-`ink`-plus-contrast-test model
  (DEC-089), noting `water`'s `ink`/`ref` are both `null`.
- **`README.md`** — the "Terrain and contents" section's art-authoring bullet rewritten around
  `ink` instead of `hexOverlayTone`/`HEX_OVERLAY_DARK`/`HEX_OVERLAY_LIGHT`, and now notes
  `water`'s no-overlay case.
- **`PLAN.md`** §2: WI-120's row removed (queue now empty); narrative paragraphs updated in
  place to record both WI-119 and WI-120 as closed. **`PLAN-COMPLETED.md`** §3: row added.
  **`INTAKE.md`**: IN-114 moved from §1.1 to §1.2, closed.

## Visible behavior changes

- A hex crawl's terrain palette goes from 20 authored kinds to 41 (56 rows total with aliases
  and untouched kinds), all with new art for the 34 genuinely new shapes and the 6 superseded
  ones, each in an authored colour rather than a two-grey overlay tint. `water` now renders as
  a plain background colour with no overlay glyph. The hex-tile quick sheet's terrain swatches
  (`HexTilePanel`) and the Terrain tool's toolbar preview (`MapToolbar`) both reflect the new
  art and colours.
- A hex already painted with `grass`, `ice-floe`, `palm`, `plateau`, `scrub` or `tundra` now
  renders with the art and ink of the kind it aliases (`grassland`, `snowfields`, `jungle`,
  `hills`, `cactus`, `snowfields` respectively) rather than its own former glyph — the stored
  `kind` string is unchanged (RULE-007), only what it resolves to.
- No new schema, no migration, no store-contract change, no rules change.

## Deviations

1. **The contrast guard (`HEX_TERRAIN_CONTRAST_MIN`, 3.3) is scoped to the 40 authored kinds
   with an overlay (the 41 candidates minus `water`), not to every row with an `ink`.** DEC-089
   itself scopes its mechanism there ("the ~42 concrete pairs remain WI-119's deliverable").
   Applying it universally was tried first and failed on two groups WI-119 did not check it
   against: the 8 untouched kinds' baked-in `ink` (computed from the retired `hexOverlayTone`,
   a materially weaker luminance-gap guarantee — `mountain-major` measures 1.96:1,
   `tree-deciduous` 1.87:1) and, more marginally, some of the 6 aliases (`grass` 3.00:1,
   `scrub` 2.85:1 by this item's own arithmetic — close to but not matching WI-119 §4's
   reported 2.95/2.85, a rounding-method difference, not a disagreement). Bumping the untouched
   kinds' colours to clear 3.3 would have been a real visible change to kinds WI-119 explicitly
   asked to leave alone ("no behaviour change for anything this section doesn't touch") — so
   the guard is scoped instead, with the two exempted groups' rationale recorded in the test
   file itself and cross-referenced here.
2. **The render-box multiplier (`hexTerrainArtPx`, 1.1× → 1.8×) and the picker-retirement list
   (excluding `palm`/`plateau`/`grass`/`scrub`/`tundra`/`ice-floe` from a new-authoring picker)
   in `docs/completed/wi-119/palette.json`'s `renderRecommendation`/`pickerRecommendation` are
   *not* applied.** Both are explicitly labelled recommendations for this item to weigh, not
   part of what SPEC-047 §8 or this item's `PLAN.md` gate describes ("land the approved roster
   into `HEX_TERRAIN_CATALOG`, record Inkwell Ideas provenance" — the gate is silent on both).
   The render-box change also has no established masking precedent in this renderer (no
   existing `Sprite.mask` usage anywhere in `vector-engine.ts`) and 1.8× alone, without a
   per-hex clip, would let a wide glyph's silhouette cross into a neighbouring hex — the
   defect the existing `vector-engine-hex.test.ts` geometry test exists to prevent. Left as a
   follow-up rather than folded in under RULE-015's unblock exception, since neither is
   required to land the catalog; noted in `PLAN.md` for a future intake item.
3. **`HEX_TERRAIN_OVERLAY_ALPHA` moved from `vector-engine.ts` to `catalog.ts`** (still 0.55,
   unchanged) so the contrast guard test can reference the exact render-time alpha rather than
   a second, hand-copied constant that could drift from it. `vector-engine.ts` re-exports
   nothing new; its own local `HEX_TERRAIN_OVERLAY_ALPHA` constant was deleted and its one call
   site now reads `hexMap.HEX_TERRAIN_OVERLAY_ALPHA`.

#### WI-103 — Hex overlay storage: two collections, v29 + migration, export, rules, store contract

**Spec:** SPEC-047 §2 (annotated). **From:** IN-092, IN-094. **Model:** `opus`.

**Changes made.**

- `packages/shared/src/types.ts` — `CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION` **28 → 29**, and two new
  document types beside `HexTile`:
  - `HexSymbol { id, point, kind }` — one catalog symbol on a hex map. `point` is a
    `HexPoint` (thirds of a hex step, SPEC-047 §1), spelled structurally so `types.ts`
    stays import-free, as `HexTile.hex` is.
  - `HexLine { id, kind, points, shade, width, join }` — a road or river as an ordered run
    of `HexPoint`s, with `HexLineKind` (`road | river`) and `HexLineJoin`
    (`mitre | round`) as their own exported unions.
- `packages/shared/src/map/hex/catalog.ts` — `HexLineEntry`, `HEX_LINE_CATALOG` (three
  browns and three blues, each kind carrying the join its tool starts a line at) and
  `HEX_LINE_WIDTHS` (three multiples of `hex.size`, not pixels — RULE-006), plus
  `hexLineEntry`, `isKnownHexLine`, `hexLineShade` and `hexLineWidth`. A document carries
  the kind and an **index**; an out-of-range or non-finite index clamps rather than
  dropping the line, the posture `hexTerrainEntry` takes with an unknown kind. The file
  gains one type-only import from `types.ts` for the two unions (see **Deviations**).
- `packages/shared/src/schemas.ts` — `HexPointSchema` (finite, deliberately **not**
  `.int()`: a Hex-snapped point is integer-valued and a Free one is not, and both are
  legitimate stored positions), `HexSymbolSchema`, `HexLineSchema`, `HexLineKindSchema`,
  `HexLineJoinSchema`. `points` requires at least two; `shade`/`width` are non-negative
  integers, unbounded above so a longer future palette still parses.
- `packages/shared/src/converters.ts` — `hexSymbolConverter` and `hexLineConverter`, both
  ordinary `FirestoreDataConverter`s. Their doc comments state why they are *not*
  `hexTileFromDoc`-style pure helpers: a hex tile's id is its coordinate and a bad id has
  to be survivable, whereas these ids are opaque and the position is a field.
- `packages/shared/src/store/campaign-store.ts` — `'hexSymbols'` and `'hexLines'` join
  `EXPORTED_MAP_COLLECTIONS` (beside `hexTiles`, and for the same RULE-006 reason: axial
  geometry must not sit behind a name that promises cell units), and six methods join the
  contract: `subscribeHexSymbols` / `placeHexSymbol` / `removeHexSymbol` and
  `subscribeHexLines` / `addHexLine` / `removeHexLine`.
- `packages/shared/src/store/memory-store.ts` — `MapBucket` gains the two
  `ReactiveCollection`s and `MemoryStore` the six methods. `addHexLine` copies the points
  array rather than aliasing the caller's, which the Firebase side gets free by
  serialising. **`LocalStore` inherits all six** and needed no change (RULE-001's third
  implementation).
- `packages/shared/src/store/firebase-store.ts` — `hexSymbolCol`/`hexLineCol` (converted,
  unlike `hexTileCol`) and the six methods. `addHexLine` is one `setDoc` for the finished
  polyline: the in-progress line never leaves the drawing client, so there is no frame
  traffic and nothing goes near RTDB (RULE-003).
- `packages/shared/src/migrations/index.ts` — the **v28 → v29** entry. A no-op on the room
  doc and with no document half: the collections are new and map-scoped, so there is
  nothing anywhere to move into them, and an absent collection already means "nothing
  drawn here yet". The bump earns its keep by stamping `.vttcamp` archives.
- `firebase/firestore.rules` — `match /hexSymbols/{symbolId}` and `match
  /hexLines/{lineId}`, both `signedIn()` read and member-or-GM write, copying `hexTiles`'
  block. Two blocks rather than one wildcard, deliberately: a `match /{collection}/{id}`
  would grant a rule to every path a future build invents.
- **Tests**, in five suites:
  - `campaign-store.contract.ts` — four cases (RULE-001, run against `MemoryStore`,
    `FirebaseStore` and `LocalStore`): a snapped and a free symbol store and remove; two
    symbols share one hex without collapsing, which is the id being opaque; a road's three
    integer vertices and a river's fractional ones come back exactly, and `join` survives
    independently of `kind`; the two collections and the two maps stay isolated.
  - `map/hex/catalog.test.ts` — five cases over the line catalog: two kinds, three shades
    each, the join styles §4's table names, three increasing sub-1 widths, kind + index →
    colour (a road's index 1 is not a river's), the clamp, and the unknown-kind fallback.
  - `migrations/index.test.ts` — v28 → v29 is a no-op on the room doc, and invents neither
    collection anywhere.
  - `rules/firestore.rules.test.ts` — five cases: a member may place/edit/remove a symbol
    and draw/recolour/erase a line, the GM may do both, a non-member is denied both, and
    any signed-in client may read both.
  - `portability/vttcamp.test.ts` — the RULE-014 round trip, with the vertices asserted
    explicitly rather than left to the deep-equal: this is the test that fails loudly if a
    future serialiser starts rounding, which is what would stop two roads meeting at a
    corner.
- `README.md` — a new "Hex overlays — symbols, roads and rivers (SPEC-047 §2, schema v29)"
  subsection under the hex map system, and the thirds-lattice section's "Nothing stores a
  `HexPoint` yet" line corrected, since two collections now do.
- `docs/spec/SPEC-047.md` — §2 annotated with what was built, including the one place its
  wording had to be read rather than followed literally (the document id).
- `PLAN.md`, `PLAN-COMPLETED.md`, `INTAKE.md` — the ledger moves.

**Visible behavior changes.**

**None.** No UI, no tool and no rendered pixel moved — a referee sees exactly what they saw
before. The palette that draws these is SPEC-047 §§3–4 (WI-104, WI-105). What changed
underneath: a new room is created at `schemaVersion` 29 rather than 28, an older room is
walked forward on read by a no-op step, and a `.vttcamp` written by this build stamps 29.

**How to verify.**

- `pnpm verify` — lint, typecheck and the unit suite, which covers the catalog resolvers,
  the migration step and the `.vttcamp` round trip.
- `pnpm verify:all` — the above plus the rules suite (the two new `match` blocks) and the
  store suite (the contract's four new cases against `MemoryStore`, `FirebaseStore` and
  `LocalStore`) against the emulator.
- By hand, in a hex map's room: nothing to look at. The collections have no producer until
  WI-105, which is the point of the ordering.

**What the suites actually did**, since a summary written before verification is a
prediction rather than a record. `pnpm verify` green. `pnpm verify:all`: `test:unit`,
`test:rules` and `test:store` all passed — the three carrying this item's own coverage, and
they gate the `&&` chain, so e2e only ran because they did — and **one e2e failed**,
`hex-map.spec.ts:176` ("a hex with a note shows it on hover"), timing out for the full 180s
waiting to click a `vector-tool-select` that was still `disabled` with `title="Select
(locked — switch to Edit)"`: the map sat in `mapMode === 'view'` for the whole test.
**Re-running that spec alone passed all four of its cases**, which is the one re-run this
took. The cause is `switchToEditMode` in `apps/web/tests/e2e/helpers.ts` — it reads
`aria-pressed` once, clicks conditionally, and never asserts the mode it exists to
establish, so a click swallowed by a not-yet-wired control fails three minutes later at an
unrelated locator. **Logged as IN-107, not fixed here** (RULE-015: this work item is hex
overlay storage, and no `apps/web` file is in its diff).

**Deviations.**

- **The document id is minted, not the point's key.** §2 says each document is "positioned
  by a `HexPoint`", and the obvious reading — key it by `hexPointKey`, as `hexTiles` is
  keyed by `axialKey` — cannot hold: a Free-snap point is fractional and has no key at all
  (SPEC-047 §1 has `parseHexPointKey` reject a fractional string rather than round it onto
  the lattice), and a coordinate id would also collapse two symbols in one hex to one
  document, which §4's "one catalog symbol per click" does not ask for. So the id is minted
  the way `placeSymbol`'s is and the point is a stored field. §2 is annotated in place
  rather than rewritten.
- **`catalog.ts` gains one type-only import from `types.ts`** — `HexLineKind` and
  `HexLineJoin`. `map/hex/` had imported nothing outside itself. The alternative was to
  declare the two unions twice, in two files that must agree and that no test can be
  written to keep in step. The import is types-only, erased at build, and does not touch
  the property the code actually states, which is that **`types.ts`** imports nothing; nor
  does it make the catalog any less pure or store-free.
- **Three widths as multiples of `hex.size`, and their values, were chosen here.** §§2 and
  4 say "three widths, increasing" and nothing more. `[0.08, 0.14, 0.22]` × the
  circumradius; the unit is what RULE-006 forces (no stored pixel, one multiplier crossed
  once), and the values are pinned only as "three, increasing, between 0 and 1" so WI-105
  may retune them against real art without a schema change. The six shades were chosen the
  same way.
- **IN-107 raised, not fixed.** The verification run turned up a race in an e2e helper (see
  above). Logging it is the disposal RULE-015 prescribes; fixing it would have been a
  "while I was in there" edit, and it is not required to unblock this item — the re-run
  established that.
- **No update method.** §2 mentions a river "re-coloured" to justify storing `join` on the
  document, but the only producers §4 describes are place-one and commit-one. A setter with
  no caller would be speculative; `removeHexSymbol`/`removeHexLine` are here because
  without a delete the collections could only grow, which §2's "erased and never drawn are
  the same state" rules out.

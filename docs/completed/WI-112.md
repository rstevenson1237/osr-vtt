# WI-112 — Eye and Ping aimed at a token — click-time resolution, dropped when the token moves

**Spec:** SPEC-046 §2 · **From:** IN-087 · **Model:** `sonnet` · **Effort:** S–M · **Closed:**
2026-09-09

Either View tool (Eye, Ping) may now pick a token rather than open floor. A click on a
token's sprite resolves it at click time and aims the mark at that token's current
position instead of the raw pointer position. Nothing about the target is published — a
ping still writes only `{ id, uid, x, y, ts }` — so a token ping is a floor ping that
happened to be aimed at a token's position, resolved independently by every client from
its own render state.

## Changes made

- **`apps/web/src/lib/components/VectorMapView.svelte`**
  - `attachDragHandlers`'s `pointerdown` handler now special-cases `tool === 'ping'` and
    `tool === 'eye'` before the existing select-and-drag logic: a ping publishes at the
    clicked token's `pos` (`store.publishPing(roomId, token.pos)`); an eye is placed at
    the clicked token's position converted to lattice units (`token.pos.x / cellSize`,
    `.../ cellSize`) and starts its usual countdown. Both branches `stopPropagation` and
    return, so a token click under these tools no longer selects or drags it.
  - New `tokenRadiusPx(token)` and `tokenAt(p)` helpers (pixel-space token hit-test),
    used only to resolve a *received* ping's target on first sight — an aim click itself
    already knows which token it landed on, since the token's own sprite receives the
    pointer event.
  - New `livePings` state fed by `subscribePings` (replacing the previous direct
    `engine?.renderPings(p)` call), a `pingTargets` map remembering each ping's resolved
    target (`null` = floor ping) the first time it's seen, and `resolvePingsForRender`,
    which turns the raw feed into engine-ready pings: a floor ping unchanged, a
    token-aimed ping annotated with `tokenRingRadius`, or dropped entirely once its
    remembered token's committed position no longer matches the ping's point (SPEC-046
    §2's "moved off the mark").
  - A new `$effect` re-runs `resolvePingsForRender`/`renderPings` whenever `livePings`
    changes *or* any token's committed `pos` changes, so a drop takes effect on the next
    move, not just the next time the ping list itself updates.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/map/vector-engine.ts`**
  - New exported `RenderPing` type (`PingPos` plus an optional `tokenRingRadius`) is what
    `renderPings` now accepts, in place of a bare `PingPos[]`.
  - `drawPing`/`tickPings`/`renderPings` carry `tokenRingRadius` per sprite. When present,
    the ring's resting radius is `tokenRingRadius + PING_TOKEN_RING_GAP` (4px) instead of
    the floor ping's fixed `PING_RADIUS_END` (5px), and it still shrinks from a radius
    `PING_RADIUS_START - PING_RADIUS_END` (9px) larger down to that resting radius over
    the ping's life — the same curve and stroke/colour as a floor ping, re-anchored to sit
    just outside the token's disc rather than at a fixed small radius.

## Visible behavior changes

- **Ping aimed at a token**: clicking a token with the Ping tool drops a ring concentric
  with the token (its radius plus a small gap) instead of at the raw click point, and the
  ring disappears early if the token moves before the ping's normal 3s lifetime ends.
- **Eye aimed at a token**: clicking a token with the Eye tool places the sightline origin
  at the token's exact position rather than wherever the pointer landed inside/near it.
- **Clicking a token with Eye or Ping no longer selects or drags it** — a View-tool click
  is now a pure "aim here" gesture, matching how it already behaved on open floor.
- A ping or eye click on open floor is unchanged.

## How to verify

- `pnpm verify` — lint + typecheck + unit, all green.
- By eye: open a multiplayer room with a token placed. Select **Ping**, click the token —
  a ring appears concentric with it (not at the exact pixel clicked) and fades over ~3s;
  drag the token away mid-ping and confirm the ring disappears before its 3s is up. Click
  open floor with Ping and confirm the ordinary small ring still appears at the exact
  click point. Repeat with **Eye**: click a token and confirm the sightline is drawn from
  the token's position; click floor and confirm nothing changed from before.

## Deviations

- **The token-ring's gap and "pulse" delta are new numbers, not cited by the spec.**
  SPEC-046 §2 says "the token's radius plus a small gap" and "pulses inward" without
  fixing either number. `PING_TOKEN_RING_GAP = 4` and reusing the floor ping's existing
  9px shrink delta (`PING_RADIUS_START - PING_RADIUS_END`) were chosen to read as the same
  gesture at a different anchor, per the spec's own framing ("the two read as one
  feature"), and are not load-bearing on any other behavior.
- **Drop detection compares each ping's remembered token against its last *committed*
  position**, not a live mid-drag position (which other clients only see via ephemeral
  RTDB drag frames, not the `tokens` roster). A token dragged and dropped elsewhere while
  a ping tracks it will show the drop once the move commits, not the instant the drag
  starts. The spec explicitly accepts client disagreement here ("bounded by the
  three-second life, breaks no state"), and this keeps the feature entirely within the
  existing `tokens` reactivity rather than wiring a new consumer onto the drag-frame
  stream.

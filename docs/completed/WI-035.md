## WI-035 — Battle map: bounded camera, doubled grid density, view-tools-only toolbar filter

**Spec:** SPEC-029 §4 · **From:** IN-010 · **Closed:** 2026-08-09 · `claude-code`, `opus`,
effort `high`

### Changes made

- **`apps/web/src/lib/map/battle-map.ts`** (new) — the one place SPEC-029 §4's three
  differences come from, all **derived from `GameMap.battle` at render time, not stored**:
  `isBattleMap`, `gridSquaresPerCell` (2 or 1), `gridStepPx` (`cellSize / squares`),
  `displayPerSquare` (`perSquare / squares`) and `battleCameraBounds` (the captured rect
  times `cellSize`). The rect is already held in the *source* map's lattice units
  (RULE-006), so a battle map shares that lattice space; a stored grid step or per-square
  value expressed in some other space would contradict it. Pure — no Pixi, no Svelte.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/map/battle-map.test.ts`** (new) — each rule on its own, plus the one
  that keeps the other two honest: a doubled square count against a halved per-square
  value is the same ground, so a measured distance does not move.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/map/pan-zoom.ts`** — `CameraBounds`/`CameraView` types,
  `clampCameraToBounds` (a scale floor plus a per-axis pan clamp, centring on an axis the
  scaled rect is too short to fill) and `fitCameraToBounds`. `setupPanZoom` gained a sixth
  optional `clampCamera` callback, invoked after each of the three places it writes the
  world transform directly — drag-pan, wheel zoom, two-finger pan/pinch. Unbounded maps
  pass the default no-op, so nothing changes for them.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/map/pan-zoom.test.ts`** (new) — the clamp holds every edge in both
  directions, will not zoom out past the whole rect, takes the axis needing the most
  zoom-out as its floor, centres an axis the rect cannot fill, and leaves the view alone
  both for a degenerate rect and before the canvas has been laid out (Pixi reports a 0 × 0
  screen until the host element has a size).
- **`apps/web/src/lib/map/vector-engine.ts`** — `setCameraBounds(bounds | null)` and
  `fitCamera()` on `VectorMapEngine`, a module-local `cameraBounds` + `clampCameraNow()`
  wired into `setupPanZoom`, and the `ResizeObserver` now re-imposes the bound before
  redrawing (a taller canvas can expose ground that was legally off screen a moment ago).
- **`apps/web/src/lib/map/tool-groups.ts`** — `VIEW_TOOL_IDS`, read off the `view` group
  rather than listed a second time, so the palette and the battle map's subset cannot
  drift apart.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/map/tool-groups.test.ts`** — `VIEW_TOOL_IDS` is exactly
  `['pan','eye','measure','ping']`, all pass `isViewTool`, and nothing that carves,
  overlays or selects is in it.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/shell/map-tool-controller.svelte.ts`** — `isBattleMap` state and
  `setBattleMap(on)`, which carries the active-tool rule the way `setMapMode` does:
  entering a battle map forces a carve/edit tool back to Pan, so none can stay armed on a
  canvas whose palette no longer offers it. Cleared in `release()`.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/shell/map-tool-controller.test.ts`** — `setBattleMap` forces Pan from
  a carve tool, leaves an already-active view tool alone, and leaving a battle map clears
  the flag without touching the tool; `release()` drops the flag.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/components/MapToolbar.svelte`** — new `toolSubset` prop. The
  `visibleGroups` derived now applies two filters over the one catalog (the existing
  referee-only `capture` gate, and the subset) and **drops a group left with no tools**,
  so a battle map's palette shows one row rather than four empty ones with their group
  rules. Tools outside the subset are hidden, not disabled — unlike the Edit/View soft
  lock, they are never coming back on this map.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/components/shell/MapToolPalette.svelte`** — `toolSubset` passed
  straight through; the palette itself has no opinion on which tools a map offers.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/components/shell/sheets/MapToolsSheet.svelte`** — where the choice is
  made: `controller.isBattleMap ? VIEW_TOOL_IDS : null`. It is the one component that
  knows both what is on stage and what the palette renders.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/components/VectorMapView.svelte`** — `battleMap`/`gridCellSize`/
  `cameraBounds` deriveds; `renderAll` feeds `gridCellSize` to `renderGrid` (everything
  else still takes `cellSize` — the lattice space is untouched); `onMount` sets the bound
  after restoring the remembered camera and calls `fitCamera()` when there is none;
  two new `$effect`s mirror `setBattleMap` to the controller and keep the bound in step
  with the map on stage; the `measure-summary` readout goes through `displayPerSquare`.
- **`README.md`** — "Battle maps" gained the three render-time differences and why each is
  derived rather than stored; the Tools section notes that the camera is unbounded with
  exactly one exception, and that `MapToolbar` renders `TOOL_GROUPS` in full unless a
  caller narrows it.
- **`docs/spec/SPEC-029.md`** — status line (WI-035 landed, WI-036 is all that remains),
  and §4 now records that the three differences are derived, that the zoom floor is the
  *fit* scale rather than the *cover* scale and why, and that measured distances do not

### Visible behavior changes

**None reachable yet.** All three differences key on `GameMap.battle`, and nothing writes
that field — WI-036's Start button is what creates a temporary battle map. Every ordinary
map renders, measures and pans exactly as before: `gridStepPx` returns `cellSize`,
`displayPerSquare` returns the stored value, and `battleCameraBounds` returns `null`, which
leaves `setupPanZoom` on its no-op clamp.

Once a battle map exists, a viewer on one will find: the camera cannot leave the captured
rect and cannot zoom out past the whole of it (opening on a fit of the rect when they have
no remembered camera for it); a grid at twice the density, with the `measure-summary`
readout halved to match what one drawn square is now worth; and a Map tools sheet showing
only the View row — Pan, Eye, Measure, Ping — with the active tool dropped to Pan on
arrival if it was anything else. Measured distances read the same in feet as they did on
the source map.

### Deviations

- **The camera's zoom floor is the *fit* scale, not the *cover* scale.** SPEC-029 §4 says
  only "the camera clamps to the captured rect". Read strictly — never show a pixel
  outside the rect — a capture whose aspect ratio differs from the viewer's canvas could
  never be seen whole, which is the first thing a referee starting a fight wants. The floor
  is therefore the scale that fits the rect, and full zoom-out letterboxes on the looser
  axis. My first implementation took the strict reading; the new `pan-zoom` tests caught it
  before anything else did. §4 has been amended to record the choice.
- **`RoomMeasure.perSquare` halves for display only.** `measureSpanText`/
  `strokeMeasureText` multiply a span in *lattice cells* by the stored `perSquare`, which
  is the true distance on a battle map as much as anywhere else. Halving the value they
  consume, without also doubling the cell count, would have made the Measure tool report
  half the real distance. So the halving lands where the value means "what one drawn square
  is worth" — the `measure-summary` readout — and the measuring path is untouched.
- **`gridSettings.subdivide` is passed through unchanged**, so a battle map cut from a
  source with the 10′/5′ dual-mark style draws quarter-cell interlines over its own
  half-cell grid. §4 does not mention subdivide, and suppressing a per-map display setting
  the referee chose seemed the more surprising of the two behaviours.
- **`INTAKE.md`'s IN-010 row was not moved from §1.1 to §1.2** — the same call WI-033 and
  WI-034 both recorded. IN-010 covers WI-033 – WI-036 as one Shape-A item and WI-036 is
  still open, so closing the row now would misreport the feature as delivered. It already
  carries a note flagging it as correctly open despite landed work items pointing at it.
  It moves when WI-036 lands.

  quoted above is a clean one from the settled tree.

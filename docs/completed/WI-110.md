# WI-110 — `corridorPoly`'s Free zero-length case commits a `bandWidth` square

**Spec:** SPEC-028 §4 (amended) · **From:** IN-108 · **Agent:** `claude-code` · **Model:**
`sonnet` · **Effort:** S · **Closed:** 2026-09-08

## Changes made

- `packages/shared/src/map/vector/primitives.ts` — `corridorPoly` now checks the
  `!legs.length` case (only reachable under Free snap, where `bandSpan` has no cell to
  extend a same-point leg to) and, under Free, falls back to a `width × width` square
  centred on the click via the existing `cornerBlock` helper, instead of returning `[]`.
  Cell and Half snap were already unaffected — their `bandSpan` extends a same-point leg
  to the full pointed-at cell, so a click there already committed one cell of corridor.
- `packages/shared/src/map/vector/primitives.test.ts` — added
  `'a click with no drag under Free snap is one bandWidth square, not nothing'`, mirroring
  the existing Cell-snap click test.
- `docs/spec/SPEC-028.md` §4 — amendment note recording the fix and confirming the Free
  indicator needed no change: WI-107 already made `targetedBandRect` show the same square
  under Free (§6), so IN-108's "plus the Free indicator" half of the request was already
  shipped before this item ran.

## Visible behavior changes

- Drawing a Corridor under Free snap with a click and no drag now carves a `bandWidth ×
  bandWidth` square at the click point, instead of carving nothing. Every other snap mode
  (Cell, Half) and every non-zero-length drag are unchanged.

## How to verify

- `pnpm verify` (lint + typecheck + unit) — green.
- Unit test: `packages/shared/src/map/vector/primitives.test.ts` → "a click with no drag
  under Free snap is one bandWidth square, not nothing".
- Manually: Corridor tool, Free snap, click once without dragging — a square of the
  current band width appears on the map instead of nothing.

## Deviations

None. The Free indicator part of the original description was already correct as
shipped by WI-107 (SPEC-028 §6); the gate's own note said to re-read the code as WI-107
left it rather than as IN-108's entry described it, and this item's actual code change is
scoped to `corridorPoly` alone.

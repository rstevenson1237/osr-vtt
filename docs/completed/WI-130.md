## WI-130 — The terrain overlay is clipped into its art

**Status: Completed 2026-09-14**

SPEC-047 §13. The terrain overlay's box grows from `size * 1.22` to `size * 1.8` — WI-119's
figure, the one that was actually looked at against reference material — and each kind's art is
composited against a hex silhouette **once, at load time**, so nothing is clipped per frame and
the overlay layer stays the single batched sprite draw WI-122 measured at 0.20 ms/frame. This is
DEC-092 (b), and it delivers what SPEC-047 §9 wanted and WI-122 could not: the stencil mask §9
approved cost 376 ms/frame for the same picture, and the fallback that shipped instead (a smaller
box, unclipped) was 11% over the 1.1× it replaced where the study asked for 64%.

### Changes made

- **`packages/shared/src/map/hex/catalog.ts`** — added `HEX_TERRAIN_ART_SCALE = 1.8` and
  `hexTerrainClipPolygon()`, beside `HEX_TERRAIN_OVERLAY_ALPHA`. The polygon is expressed in the
  art box's own unit square (box = 1, centre `0.5,0.5`, hex circumradius `1 / 1.8` of the box's
  side), so it is size- and resolution-independent and serves both the canvas bake and a CSS
  `clip-path` from one definition. Flat-top, corners due east and west, matching `hexCorners`.
  Two of its six corners fall outside the unit square by construction — that is the accepted
  uncovered-tips property, documented on the constant.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/map/vector-engine.ts`**:
  - `hexTerrainArtPx` is now `size * hexMap.HEX_TERRAIN_ART_SCALE` (1.8×, was 1.22×), and the
    "Painted hexes" block comment is rewritten: it explained why the box had to fit the hex, and
    now explains why it deliberately does not and what holds the boundary instead.
  - New `bakeHexClippedArt(img)` — module-level: `drawImage` onto a 256px offscreen canvas
    (`HEX_TERRAIN_BAKE_PX`), then `globalCompositeOperation = 'destination-in'` and a fill of
    `hexTerrainClipPolygon()` scaled to that canvas. Drawing at an explicit size rather than the
    source's own reported one is WI-125's lesson carried over.
  - New `loadHexClippedTexture(ref)` with its own `hexClippedTextureCache`, kept separate from
    `artTextureCache` because the two hold different pictures of the same file — a contents icon
    or a placed symbol still wants the raw glyph. One bake per terrain _kind_, not per painted
    hex. Loaded via `loadImageElement` (newly imported from `../tokens/texture-load`) rather than
    `PIXI.Assets.load`, because the bake needs an `HTMLImageElement` to `drawImage`.
  - `syncHexArt` gains a `load` parameter defaulting to `loadCachedTexture`; `renderHexTiles`
    passes `loadHexClippedTexture` for the terrain layer only. Contents and symbols are unchanged.
  - The texture-load promise in `syncHexArt` gains a `.catch` that warns and leaves the sprite
    non-renderable. Required by the new loader — a rejected `loadImageElement` would otherwise be
    an unhandled rejection where `PIXI.Assets.load` was previously the only source.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/components/shell/HexTilePanel.svelte`** — the palette swatch is clipped to
  match the tile (§13's first named consequence, taken by agreeing rather than by a disclaimer).
  `overlayStyle` appends a `clip-path` built from `hexTerrainClipPolygon()` at module scope
  (`OVERLAY_CLIP`), and `.art.overlay` grows from the shared 74% inset to `100%`/`100%` so the
  swatch square _is_ the art box, exactly as on the map. `.art`'s 74% stays for contents icons,
  which really do sit on top of the terrain. No testid, markup or handler changes;
  `pointer-events: none` on `.art` is unchanged, so the enlarged span does not intercept clicks.
- **`apps/web/src/lib/map/vector-engine-hex.test.ts`** — the fit assertion becomes a clip
  assertion, as §9 anticipated and §13 requires. Replaced "keeps a terrain overlay inside its own
  hex" and "takes that fit right to its bound" with: the box is `1.8 × size`; its half-diagonal is
  now _greater_ than the inradius (the old invariant, inverted deliberately); art at the four box
  corners (`1.27 × size`) is outside the clip, a point 1% past the inradius is outside and one 1%
  inside is kept; the polygon equals `hexCorners`' own six vertices (the hex itself, not an
  inscribed circle); and the box's half-width is below `size`, pinning the uncovered east/west
  tips as a recorded decision rather than a surprise. The contents-icon fit assertion is kept
  unchanged — it is still unclipped.
- **Docs** — `README.md` (hex terrain overlay bullet: the bake, the two visible consequences, the
  bake-time-fixed caveat, the swatch agreement), `docs/spec/SPEC-047.md` §13 (a "Landed" note
  naming the mechanism and the test), `PLAN.md`, `PLAN-COMPLETED.md`, `INTAKE.md`.

### Visible behavior changes

Yes — this is a deliberate change in the map's character, written down in SPEC-047 §13 ahead of
time rather than arrived at.

- **Terrain art is much larger and painted hexes read edge-to-edge.** The overlay grows from
  1.22× to 1.8× the hex's circumradius (+48% on a side), so adjacent painted hexes touch and the
  map reads as a mosaic of textures rather than glyphs floating on a colour field. The 55%
  overlay alpha is unchanged, so each hex's `color` still reads through.
- **The hex's east and west corner tips show bare `color`.** The box's half-width is `0.9 × size`
  and a hex corner is at `1.0 × size`, so the art never reaches them. Accepted at 1.8× — 2.0×
  would cover them and was not chosen (DEC-092, amended by the user 2026-09-11).
- **Nothing outside a hex is drawn.** The box's corners now sit well outside the hex on the
  diagonal; the bake makes them transparent, so no glyph bleeds onto a neighbour.
- **The Terrain palette swatch matches.** Each swatch's art now fills its button and is clipped
  to the same hex, so it previews the painted tile instead of showing a differently-shaped
  sample of the ink.
- Nothing stored changes, no schema bump, no migration (RULE-007 untouched). No testid moved,
  renamed or removed (RULE-005). No store method added or changed (RULE-001).

### How to verify

1. `pnpm verify` — lint, typecheck, unit. Green.
2. `pnpm vitest run apps/web/src/lib/map/vector-engine-hex.test.ts` for the clip assertions alone.
3. Open a hex map, pick the Terrain tool, and paint a run of adjacent hexes: the art fills each
   hex to its flats, neighbours touch, and the east/west corner tips stay the flat `color`.
   Nothing crosses a hex boundary.
4. Open the hex-tile quick sheet and compare a Terrain swatch against a hex painted with that
   kind — same silhouette, same framing.
5. Pan and zoom over a heavily painted map: smooth, because nothing is masked per frame.
   `apps/web/bench/hex-overlay-cost.ts` is the harness WI-122 used if a figure is wanted.

### Deviations

- **The emulator battery could not be run in this session.** `pnpm verify` (lint + typecheck +
  unit) is green locally, but `pnpm verify:all` and any targeted Playwright run need the Firebase
  emulators and the `firebase` CLI is not installed in this sandbox
  (`Failed to run the Firebase CLI: spawn firebase ENOENT`). CI's `test-emulators` job runs the
  full suite on the PR. The e2e exposure was checked by hand instead: the only specs touching this
  work are `hex-map.spec.ts`'s `hex-terrain-forest` clicks and `aria-pressed` assertions, and the
  swatch keeps its testid, its markup and its `pointer-events: none` art span, so the enlarged
  overlay cannot intercept those clicks.
- **The `.catch` added to `syncHexArt`'s texture-load promise** is an unblocking change under the
  RULE-015 exception rather than part of the approved scope. `loadHexClippedTexture` can reject
  where `PIXI.Assets.load` was previously the only source on that path; without the handler a
  missing or unreadable terrain asset would surface as an unhandled promise rejection. Behaviour
  on failure is unchanged — the sprite stays non-renderable, which is what it already was.
- **`HEX_TERRAIN_ART_SCALE` and `hexTerrainClipPolygon()` live in shared**, not in
  `vector-engine.ts` beside `hexTerrainArtPx`, because §13's swatch consequence needs the same
  geometry in a Svelte component and importing `vector-engine` there would pull PIXI into the
  panel. This is an added export on a catalog module, not a store-contract change (RULE-001).

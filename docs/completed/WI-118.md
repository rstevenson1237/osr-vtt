# WI-118 — A dragged token's ring, disc and badges follow it

**Spec:** — (defect fix) · **From:** IN-112 · **Agent:** `claude-code` · **Model:**
`sonnet` · **Effort:** S · **Closed:** 2026-09-08

## Changes made

- `apps/web/src/lib/components/VectorMapView.svelte` — added `resyncTokenDecorations`,
  which repositions a token's background disc (`backgroundsByToken`), status ring
  (`ringsByToken`), away badge (`awayBadgesByToken`) and broken-image badge
  (`brokenImageBadgesByToken`) to the sprite's current position. A token is five separate
  display objects with no shared container; the drag handlers previously moved only the
  sprite, leaving the other four behind until the next full `syncSprites` pass. The new
  function is called from both sites: after every `globalpointermove` update during a live
  drag, and after the drop-time snap. It repositions only, not fill/stroke/visibility.

## Visible behavior changes

- Dragging a token now keeps its status ring, colour disc and any away/broken-image
  corner badges glued to the sprite for the whole gesture and at the drop point, instead
  of leaving them at the pick-up position until some unrelated re-render caught them up.

## How to verify

- `pnpm verify` (lint + typecheck + unit) — green.
- Manually: place a token with a colour (so its background disc is visible), give it a
  status ring (select it, or have it own the seat), and drag it across the map — the disc
  and ring move with the sprite throughout the drag and land with it on drop. Repeat with
  a disconnected player's token (away badge) or a token with a bad `imageRef` (broken
  badge) to confirm those follow too.

## Deviations

None. No dedicated unit-test infrastructure exists for this component's Pixi rendering;
verification here is `pnpm verify` plus the manual check above.

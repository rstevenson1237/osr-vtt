# WI-122 — The terrain overlay's render box

**Spec:** SPEC-047 §9 · **From:** IN-115 · **Agent:** `claude-code` · **Model:** `opus` ·
**Effort:** M · **Closed:** 2026-09-11

**Outcome in one line:** the approved 1.8×-plus-per-hex-clip was implemented and measured,
it failed DEC-090's cost condition by three orders of magnitude, and the pre-approved
fallback shipped — `hexTerrainArtPx` is `size * 1.22`, unclipped.

## Changes made

- `apps/web/src/lib/map/vector-engine.ts` — `hexTerrainArtPx` goes from `size * 1.1` to
  `size * 1.22`, the largest centred square that still fits inside a flat-top hex
  (`box·√2/2 < size·√3/2` ⟹ `box < size·√(3/2)` = 1.2247·size). The section comment records
  why the box is capped there rather than at §9's 1.8×, with the measured figures.
  `hexContentsArtPx` (0.9×) and `HEX_TERRAIN_OVERLAY_ALPHA` (0.55) are untouched, as §9
  requires. **No mask, and no other render-pass change**: the per-hex clip was built,
  verified green and then reverted once measured — it survives only in the harness below.
- `apps/web/src/lib/map/vector-engine-hex.test.ts` — the existing fit assertion is **kept**
  (§9 would have replaced it with a clip assertion; with no clip, it is still the thing
  holding "a glyph belongs to exactly one hex"). Added a second assertion pinning the box
  within 1% of the geometric ceiling from both sides, so the box cannot drift up past what
  fits without someone deciding on a clipping mechanism.
- `apps/web/bench/hex-overlay-cost.ts`, `apps/web/bench/hex-overlay-cost.html`,
  `apps/web/bench/run.mjs` — the render-cost harness DEC-090's condition required. Rebuilds
  `renderHexTiles`' layers over 64 and 400 painted hexes, times five variants at rest and
  under pan/zoom, and prices the stencil separately from the fill. Dev-only: it is not an
  entry point of either build, and no `src/` module imports it.
- `apps/web/tsconfig.json`, `eslint.config.js` — bring `bench/**` under typecheck and give
  `apps/web/bench/*.mjs` Node globals, so the harness is held to the same bar as the rest.
- `docs/completed/wi-122/render-cost.md` — the measurement: method, environment and its
  limits, both runs' figures, and what they rule out.
- `docs/spec/SPEC-047.md` §9 — records the measured result, that DEC-090 (b) applies, and
  one correction to the section's own arithmetic (the fallback is 1.2247×, not "≈1.4×").
  Marks §9's goal as still open rather than delivered.
- `README.md` — the painted-hex bullet now names both boxes' sizes, says the box rather
  than a clip is what keeps a glyph in one hex, and why 1.22× is a ceiling and not a taste.
- `INTAKE.md` §1.1 — **IN-118** logged: a per-hex clip that batches (the hex as drawn
  geometry, not a stencil), which is what §9's goal actually needs.

## Visible behavior changes

- A terrain overlay glyph on a hex map is drawn **11% larger** (1.1× → 1.22× the hex's
  circumradius). Nothing else moves: the contents icon, the overlay's 55% alpha, the
  per-region fill and every stored field are unchanged. The change is deliberately small —
  see Deviations.
- No change to any build output, CLI output or file the app writes.

## How to verify

- `pnpm verify` (lint + typecheck + unit) — green, including the two fit assertions in
  `apps/web/src/lib/map/vector-engine-hex.test.ts`.
- `pnpm verify:all` (adds rules/store/e2e against the emulator) — see Deviations for the
  result in this environment.
- The measurement: `node apps/web/bench/run.mjs` (starts its own Vite server on 5199, needs
  no emulator) prints the per-variant JSON. Expect unmasked variants under ~1 ms/frame and
  masked variants in the tens-to-hundreds of ms, whatever the absolute scale of the host.
- Visually: open a hex map, paint several adjacent hexes with different terrain, and check
  that each glyph is a little larger than before and that none of them crosses a hex seam.

## Deviations

1. **The approved approach was not the one that shipped — and that was pre-approved.**
   SPEC-047 §9 specifies a 1.8× box with each glyph masked to its own hex; DEC-090 answered
   (a) _conditional on cost_ with (b), "the largest box that still fits", as the recorded
   fallback. The clip was implemented in full (a `PIXI.Graphics` hexagon per painted tile,
   cached across redraws, rebuilt only on a hex-size change) and `pnpm verify` was green on
   it. It then measured at **376.60 ms/frame under pan at 400 painted hexes** against
   **0.20 ms/frame for the same 1.8× box unclipped** — the isolating variant that shows the
   cost is the stencil and not the fill — with 900 hexes unable to finish 48 frames in
   fifteen minutes. The mechanism explains it independently of the software renderer the
   numbers came from: Pixi's `StencilMaskPipe` breaks the batch four times per masked
   sprite. So (b) applies, and §9's fit assertion was kept rather than replaced.
2. **The fallback is 1.2247×, not §9's "≈1.4×", and is therefore nearly a no-op.** 1.4×
   does not fit a hex — `box·√2/2 < size·√3/2` caps a centred square at `size·√(3/2)`. The
   shipped 1.22× is 11% over the 1.1× it replaces, where WI-119 §9 asked for 64% more so
   the glyph would read as the hex's texture rather than as a speck beside the contents
   icon. **§9's goal is not delivered by this work item**, and the measurement says the
   goal is cheap under a clip that batches rather than one that stencils. Logged as IN-118
   rather than folded in (RULE-015). This is flagged rather than quietly absorbed because
   the gate was cleared on the expectation that the fallback would still be a visible step
   up, and it is not.
3. **Two config files were touched to hold the harness to the project's bar** —
   `apps/web/tsconfig.json` gains `bench/**/*.ts`, `eslint.config.js` gives
   `apps/web/bench/*.mjs` Node globals. Required to land the measurement as reviewable code
   rather than as numbers to be taken on trust, so made under RULE-015's unblocking
   exception.

# WI-123 — Retire the two superseded pre-pack contents glyphs

**Spec:** SPEC-047 §10 · **From:** IN-116 · **Agent:** `claude-code` · **Model:** `sonnet` ·
**Effort:** S · **Closed:** 2026-09-11

## Changes made

- `packages/shared/src/map/hex/catalog.ts` — `HexContentsEntry` gains an optional
  `paintable?: boolean` field (default true, mirroring WI-121's terrain field). The `tower`
  row's `ref` moves to `tower-keep`'s art (`hex/contents/tower-keep.svg`) and the `ruins`
  row's `ref` moves to `ruin`'s art (`hex/contents/ruin.svg`); both now carry
  `paintable: false`. Neither row's `kind` or `label` changed, so nothing stored is touched
  and no migration is owed (RULE-007). `danger` is untouched — kept pickable on its WI-040
  art per DEC-091 (c). Added `paintableHexContentsCatalog()`, mirroring
  `paintableHexTerrainCatalog()`: filters `HEX_CONTENTS_CATALOG` to drop `unknown` and any
  `paintable: false` row.
- `apps/web/src/lib/components/shell/HexTilePanel.svelte` — the contents palette
  (`CONTENTS`) now comes from `hexMap.paintableHexContentsCatalog()` instead of a
  component-local `unknown`-only filter over `HEX_CONTENTS_CATALOG`.
- `apps/web/src/lib/components/MapToolbar.svelte` — the hex Symbol tool's kind picker
  (`HEX_SYMBOL_KINDS`) now maps `hexMap.paintableHexContentsCatalog()` instead of the raw
  `HEX_CONTENTS_CATALOG` (which previously offered `unknown` too, by omission).
- `packages/shared/src/map/hex/catalog.test.ts` — new test asserting `ruins`/`tower`
  resolve to `ruin`/`tower-keep`'s art, are excluded from `paintableHexContentsCatalog()`,
  and that `danger` keeps both its own art and its palette slot.

## Visible behavior changes

- The hex-tile quick sheet's contents row and the Symbol tool's kind picker no longer offer
  `Ruins` or `Tower` (nor `Unknown`, previously offered by omission on the toolbar picker).
  A hex already carrying either kind still renders — now drawing `ruin`'s/`tower-keep`'s
  art instead of the old WI-040 glyph — since only `ref` moved, not `kind`. `Danger` is
  unaffected: still pickable, still its own art.

## How to verify

- `pnpm verify` (lint + typecheck + unit) — green.
- Manually: open a hex map, select a hex, and check the contents swatches in the quick
  sheet and in the Symbol tool's toolbar picker — neither lists `Ruins`, `Tower` or
  `Unknown`; `Danger` still appears. A hex stored with `ruins` or `tower` (e.g. from an
  existing save) now draws the pack's `ruin`/`tower-keep` glyph.

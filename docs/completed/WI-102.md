#### WI-102 — `HexPoint` in thirds: the type, the keys, the classification, the conversions, `snapHexPoint`

**Spec:** SPEC-047 §1 (annotated: SPEC-030 §1). **From:** IN-088 – IN-094. **Decision:**
DEC-081 (the axial overlay space). **Model:** `opus`.

**Changes made.**

- `packages/shared/src/map/hex/point.ts` — **new.** The thirds lattice, pure and store-free
  beside `axial.ts`:
  - `HexPoint { q, r }` in thirds of a hex step, `HEX_POINT_ORIGIN`, and
    `HEX_CORNER_OFFSETS` — DEC-081's six offsets written down as integers
    (`(2,−1) (1,1) (−1,2) (−2,1) (−1,−1) (1,−2)`), clockwise from due east in the same
    order `hexCorners` draws.
  - `hexPointKey` / `parseHexPointKey` — the canonical `"q,r"` string, integers only,
    `-0` normalised to `0` on both sides so one point never has two document ids.
  - `isHexCentre` / `isHexCorner` — the mod-3 test, sign-normalised so it holds in the
    negative quadrant, and false for both on a free (non-integer) point.
  - `hexPointToAxial` (`null` for anything that is not a centre) / `axialToHexPoint`.
  - `hexPointToPixel` / `pixelToHexPoint` — the render boundary, crossed exactly where
    `axialToPixel` already crosses it; `pixelToHexPoint` deliberately does not round.
  - `snapHexPoint` — the nearest lattice point, via `axialRound`'s cube rounding rather
    than two `Math.round`s, because the thirds lattice read in `(q, r)` *is* an integer
    axial lattice and the two disagree near a triangle's corner.
  - `hexPointAdd`, `hexPointEquals`, `hexPointCorners` — the small helpers
    `HEX_CORNER_OFFSETS` is unusable without (see **Deviations**).
- `packages/shared/src/map/hex/point.test.ts` — **new**, 22 cases in the shape
  `axial.test.ts` uses: the corner offsets reproduce `hexCorners` to 9 places over an
  81-hex patch at four sizes; adjacent hexes reach a shared corner through different
  offsets and land on the *same* `HexPoint`; the mod-3 classification over the whole patch
  and in the negative quadrant; key round-trips and the rejections; `snapHexPoint` is
  idempotent on the lattice and provably nearest against a brute-force scan of 625
  candidates at 200 scattered pixels; and two `@ts-expect-error` pins that `HexPoint` and
  `Axial` stay mutually unassignable.
- `packages/shared/src/map/hex/axial.ts` — `Axial` gains a phantom `readonly __space?:
  'axial'` brand and `HexPoint` carries `'thirds'`, which is what makes SPEC-047 §1's
  "distinct from `Axial` at the type level" real in both directions; the brand is types-only
  and never present at runtime, and an object literal `{ q, r }` still satisfies either.
  The header comment's claim that the fractional case "is never stored" is corrected in
  place, as DEC-081 said it must be, and `Axial`'s own doc comment now points at `point.ts`.
- `packages/shared/src/map/hex/index.ts` — re-exports `./point.js`, so everything above
  arrives under the existing `hexMap` namespace.
- `docs/spec/SPEC-030.md` §1 — annotated in place, the way WI-041 annotated §5: "integer"
  stays true of the addressing scheme, thirds are the same space scaled by three, RULE-006
  untouched. **Not a rule amendment** (DEC-081), so nothing was bundled into this change
  that RULE-017 forbids.
- `docs/spec/SPEC-047.md` §1 — "Built by WI-102" annotation naming what shipped.
- `README.md` → "Hex-grid maps" — a new "The thirds lattice — `HexPoint`" subsection
  (the finding, the mod-3 classification, why integers are load-bearing, the key space
  being distinct from `axialKey`'s, `snapHexPoint`'s rounding, the brand), plus one line
  in the paragraph listing what `map/hex/` holds.
- `PLAN.md` — WI-102's row removed from §2; WI-103 and WI-104 no longer marked blocked on
  it; a "has run and closed" paragraph added; three stale forward references corrected.
- `PLAN-COMPLETED.md` §3 — WI-102 row added.

**Visible behavior changes.** **None.** SPEC-047 §1 is geometry and tests: nothing stores a
`HexPoint`, no tool writes one, the palette is unchanged, and no rendered pixel moves. The
only observable difference is to a developer — `hexMap.HexPoint` and its functions exist,
and assigning an `Axial` to a `HexPoint` (or the reverse) is now a compile error.

**Deviations.**

- **Three helpers beyond §1's list.** `hexPointAdd`, `hexPointEquals` and
  `hexPointCorners`, alongside `HEX_POINT_ORIGIN` and `HEX_CORNER_OFFSETS`. The offsets
  constant *is* DEC-081's finding and is what the tests check the lattice against, and it
  cannot be applied to a hex without an add and a corners helper; equality is the operation
  the whole "integers, not floats" argument exists to make possible. All five are pure,
  three lines each, and used by the tests in this change.
- **`Axial` gained a brand.** Branding only `HexPoint` would have left `Axial` assignable
  *to* it — the wrong-scale mistake in one of its two directions. The brand is optional and
  types-only, so no existing caller changed; `HexTile.hex`, spelled `{ q, r }` in
  `types.ts`, still flows into `axialKey` unchanged.
- **`pixelToHexPoint` restates `pixelToAxial`'s formula** with the thirds factor folded in
  rather than calling it. `pixelToAxial` rounds to the nearest hex, and the unrounded value
  is precisely what a free point is.

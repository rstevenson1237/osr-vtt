## Visible behavior changes

- On a hex map, Assets → Backgrounds → Add places the image centred on where the map was last
  viewed, at its native ratio, filling the view's shorter side (previously: anchored at the
  lattice origin, sized to a square grid the hex map does not have). Fit does the same, and
  its tooltip says "Fit to the current view".
- On a hex map the row's placement readout shows hex corners (`q, r → q, r`).
- On a hex map Select can now pick, move and resize an unlocked background (previously
  unreachable — Select only picked hexes). The selection outline and eight handles show; the
  square alignment grid lines do not.
- Existing hex-map backgrounds stay exactly where they were: the referee's next open migrates
  them (v35). Square maps: no change.

## Deviations

- **§3.5 read as the grid lines only.** The rect outline and eight handles are still drawn on
  a hex map — without them §3.3's resize has nothing to grab. Recorded in SPEC-059 §3.
- **Pick order on hex maps.** Select offers a press to the backgrounds before the hex pick;
  otherwise §3.3's gesture is unreachable. A hex pick clears the background selection.
- **"Current view"** is the map's remembered camera (the panel is never on stage with the
  canvas), or the window centred on `0,0` for a map not yet opened on this client.
- **Un-migrated hex backgrounds are drawn from their old rect × `cellSize`** until the
  referee's open runs the v35 step, so a player who opens first sees them where they were.
- **Ledger reorder.** Moving `backgrounds` to v35 reordered `COLLECTION_MIGRATION_STEPS` to
  stay ascending; the background and token-letter steps touch disjoint collections.
- **Branch.** Developed on `claude/project-thread-hi3ice`, the branch this session was
  assigned, rather than `claude/wi-217`.

No unblock change and no Deviations-budget fix.

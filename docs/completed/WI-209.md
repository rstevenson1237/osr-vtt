# WI-209 — Presence-chip initials legible on Referee/Records seats

Batch 6 (WI-206 – WI-212), one pull request (RULE-016). IN-211. Model `sonnet`, effort medium. Spec: WI-175.

## Changes made

- `apps/web/src/lib/theme/tokens.css` — new `--chip-referee-bg/-ink` and `--chip-records-bg/-ink` in both themes. `parchment-dark`: fills stay the group aliases, ink is `--text` (7.8:1 / 10.0:1, was 1.8:1 / 1.4:1). `keyed-blue`: Records keeps its fill with ink `#0b1118` (5.7:1, was 3.0:1); Referee takes a deeper fill `#6a5ea0` with white ink (5.6:1, was 3.95:1) because no ink reaches 4.5:1 on the old mid-tone purple.
- `apps/web/src/lib/components/shell/SessionTab.svelte` — each chip carries its own background and ink; the other seats still use `--bg-root`. `data-testid`s unchanged.
- `apps/web/src/lib/theme/contrast.test.ts` (new) — WCAG contrast over `tokens.css` itself; this item's cases, and those of WI-210 – WI-212.

## Visible behavior changes

Referee and Records chip initials are readable; in `keyed-blue` the referee chip is a deeper purple.

## How to verify

`pnpm --filter @osr-vtt/web exec vitest run src/lib/theme/contrast.test.ts`; look at the presence chips in both themes.

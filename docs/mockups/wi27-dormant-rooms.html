WI-27 — Dormant Rooms

Mockup gate for WI-27 (spec R25, Master Plan Part III). One UI-affecting surface: the dormant affordance on a referee's own My Rooms row in the Lobby.

The governing idea is R25.2's: this is a surface, not a reaper. Nothing here deletes anything on a timer, at a threshold, or in the background. A room that has been quiet for three months is not a problem to be cleaned up — it is a campaign on hiatus — so the affordance offers the three things a referee might actually want and treats Keep as a first-class answer rather than a dismissal of a warning.

Export downloads the .vttcamp archive and does nothing else — it is the answer for "I want this off my list but not gone". Delete opens the row's existing confirm and runs the same recursive delete every other Delete runs; no new destructive code exists in this work item. Keep is silent and instant: the inset disappears and stays gone for another 90 days.

Board 3 Delete — the existing confirm, unchanged

Pressing Delete on the dormant inset is not a shortcut past the confirm. The row swaps to the same confirm strip an ordinary Delete produces, including Export & delete.

Notes for approval

Two choices worth an explicit yes/no:

1. Keep gives no feedback beyond the inset vanishing. No toast, no "kept until 12 Nov" line. The alternative is a persistent reminder that the room is old, which is the opposite of what the referee just asked for.

2. A room the referee has never opened since the feature shipped shows nothing. The activity clock is seeded at migration time, so every existing room reads as active for the first 90 days after this ships. That is deliberate (R25.1): the alternative — seeding zero, or createdAt — would greet every referee with a wall of Delete buttons for live campaigns.

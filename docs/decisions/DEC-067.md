## DEC-067 — IN-027 becomes an explicit "Tidy" action, not a change to expand

- **Question.** IN-027 asked that expanding a collapsed group grid-arrange its members
  instead of restoring their stored formation. IN-027's own record already flagged the
  destructive-write problem: `Group.memberOffsets` exists to let `collapsedDragUpdates`
  restore the formation on expand, the token layer has no undo, and a referee who collapses
  a group to drag it, then expands it, would lose the arrangement they built with no way
  back. Does the grid layout replace formation-restore, or become a separate action?
- **Recommendation.** A separate action: an explicit **Tidy** control on the group card that
  grid-arranges members on demand (4 or more wrap to a new row, per the original request),
  leaving collapse/expand exactly as it works today. This gets the requested arrangement
  without making an already-reversible-feeling gesture (expand) silently destructive, and
  `memberOffsets` keeps its one stated meaning rather than becoming half-dead.
- **Impact.** No schema change — `memberOffsets` is read exactly as before by
  `collapsedDragUpdates`; Tidy is a new one-shot write to member positions, explicit and
  named, the way any other referee action already is. Needs an origin point for the grid
  (the anchor token's cell, matching how `memberOffsets` already measures from an anchor)
  and a spacing constant, both decided at WI-082's gate rather than here.
- **Alternatives.** (a) Replace formation-restore outright, as originally asked — declined:
  makes expand destructive with no undo, which IN-027's own investigation flagged as the
  reason not to guess. (b) Close IN-027 with no work item — declined: the underlying request
  (an easy way to grid-tidy a cluttered group) is still real, just not as a side effect of
  expand.
- **Answer.** User, 2026-08-11 — separate Tidy action, as recommended.

## DEC-125 — Backgrounds on hex maps: a hex-native position, or switched off?

**Closed 2026-10-06** — answered by the user under `/work-item` (IN-069).

- **Question.** A referee can place, move and resize a background on a hex crawl exactly as
  on a square map, in square-lattice units scaled by `grid.cellSize` — a space a hex map does
  not have (RULE-006, SPEC-030 §5). What should a hex map do with backgrounds?
- **Recommendation.** **(a)** Hex-native placement: on a hex map a background stores its
  corners as `HexPoint`s (DEC-081's thirds), with `hex.size` as the render multiplier. One
  space per map, so **no RULE-006 amendment** (DEC-081 already settled that free-valued
  thirds are axial). Existing hex-map backgrounds are migrated in place (RULE-007).
- **Impact.** Changes what a stored background means on hex maps: schema bump, migration,
  `.vttcamp` round-trip, and the gesture working in axial space. Square maps unchanged.
- **Alternatives.** (b) Switch backgrounds off on hex maps: cheaper, but removes a feature
  that works by accident today, and (a) would still be owed.
- **Answer.** **(a) Hex-native** (user, 2026-10-06). SPEC-059 §3; scheduled as WI-217.

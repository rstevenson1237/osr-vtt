## DEC-115 — The referee may roll during a call, and may resolve it with who has staged

**Closed 2026-09-23** — answered by the user under `/work-item` (IN-162). **Supersedes DEC-097
in part**: players' die controls stay blocked during a call; the referee's exemption, which
DEC-097 considered and declined, is now taken, and a second exit joins Cancel.

- **Question.** Under DEC-097 (b) every die control outside an open Call for Initiative is
  disabled, the referee's included; the only exit without every seat staging is Cancel. A call
  left open by a player away from the keyboard blocks wandering-monster and reaction rolls.
- **Answer.** **Both** (user, 2026-09-23):
  1. **The referee is exempt.** `roll-button` / `roll-hidden-button` and the referee's other
     die controls stay live during a call; their rolls are ordinary `Roll`s, not staged.
     Players' controls stay blocked exactly as DEC-097 specifies.
  2. **Resolve now.** A referee-only action resolves the call with the seats that have
     staged; unstaged seats are left out of the order and can be added by hand as today.
     Nothing is rolled on anyone's behalf, so no value is produced by the app (RULE-002).
- **Impact.** SPEC-050 §3's stated behaviour changes. No schema change: Resolve now writes the
  same `resolved` status the ordinary resolve path writes, with the staged subset.
- **Alternatives.** Either half alone; keeping DEC-097 unchanged.

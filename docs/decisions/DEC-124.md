## DEC-124 — What does the background layer do when one image will not load?

**Closed 2026-10-06** — answered by the user under `/work-item` (IN-068).

- **Question.** `applyBackgrounds` loads every texture in one `Promise.all`, so one dead
  image ref stops the whole layer from updating, silently; and a remote background change
  re-places every sprite from its stored rect, snapping a sprite the local GM is dragging
  back mid-gesture. Both break SPEC-038 §2. What is the guarantee instead?
- **Recommendation.** **(a)** Each background settles independently; the rest of the layer
  always updates; an image that will not load draws nothing and its Backgrounds-panel row
  says so; a sprite under a local drag keeps its live rect until release.
- **Impact.** Restates SPEC-038 §2's render guarantee and adds one visible panel state. No
  schema or store change.
- **Alternatives.** (b) Silent everywhere (console only): the referee gets no reason for a
  vanished image. (c) A broken-image placeholder on the canvas: players see it too.
- **Answer.** **(a) Panel note** (user, 2026-10-06). SPEC-059 §2; scheduled as WI-216.

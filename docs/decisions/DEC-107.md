## DEC-107 — What the one-home-per-fact pass may delete, and what it may not

**Closed 2026-09-18** — agent default under the `/work-item` chain (IN-140). Notified at the
WI-144 gate; overridable there.

- **Question.** SPEC-052 §2 removes a closed work item's paragraph from `PLAN.md` and reduces
  two index tables to one line a row. That is a bulk deletion of prose across the project's
  ledger, and RULE-019 makes ids permanent. What exactly may go?
- **Answer.** Three things, and nothing else: (1) a "**WI-nnn has now run and closed**"
  paragraph in `PLAN.md` §2, once `docs/completed/WI-nnn.md` is confirmed to exist and to
  contain the four named sections; (2) the summary prose in a `PLAN-COMPLETED.md` §3 row,
  leaving id, title, spec, origin, agent, model, effort and date; (3) the prose in an
  `INTAKE.md` §1.2 Disposition cell, leaving the work-item ids and any spec/decision id it
  names.
- **What may not go, in any pass.** Any id, anywhere. Any `docs/completed/`, `docs/spec/` or
  `docs/decisions/` file. `INTAKE.md`'s per-batch prose sections, which its own preamble
  protects. Any ordering note, constraint or triage addendum in `PLAN.md` that still binds an
  open item. A retirement annotation (RULE-019's "annotated in place"). And anything in
  `docs/archive/**`, which RULE-020 and a hook both forbid.
- **Why a paragraph-by-paragraph check rather than a regex sweep.** The paragraphs are the only
  copy of a few facts that never made it into a record — a deviation noted in passing, a
  constraint discovered mid-run. The pass reads each one against its `docs/completed/` file and
  **moves** anything the record is missing into that record before deleting; a paragraph whose
  record does not exist is reported, not deleted.
- **Impact.** `PLAN.md` drops to roughly a tenth of its size; `PLAN-COMPLETED.md` and
  `INTAKE.md` §1.2 become scannable. Git history keeps every removed line, so the pass is
  reversible in the only sense that matters. `pnpm docs:check` (SPEC-052 §4) then holds the
  shape mechanically, which is why IN-148's item is scheduled directly behind this one.
- **Overridable**: yes, at the WI-144 gate — including the option of a smaller first pass
  (`PLAN.md` only), leaving the two index tables for later.

**Closed 2026-11-17** — answered by the user in the project thread (IN-235/IN-236).

- **Question.** SPEC-054 §3 labels every rail icon until the viewer's first rail click, §1 shows
  referees a dismissable empty-map hint card, and the `?` sheet's "Show me around" re-shows
  both. The user finds the first-start text clunky. Keep, or remove?
- **Recommendation.** Remove all three, as asked.
- **Impact.** Supersedes SPEC-054 §1 (empty-map card; "Show me around") and §3 (first-visit
  labels). The rail's icons keep their `title` tooltips and accessible names. `ShellState`
  loses `dismissedHints`, `railSeen`, `dismissHint` and `resetHints`; old `localStorage`
  values are ignored on load (stored in the browser only, so no migration). The empty-board
  hint (§1) and every other SPEC-054 section stand. No testid a Playwright spec uses is
  affected.
- **Alternatives.** Keep the cues but show them less often; keep labels on touch only (the
  original reason for §3); remove only the empty-map card.
- **Answer.** Remove the rail labels and the empty-map card, and the "Show me around" entry
  (user, 2026-11-17, in thread).

## DEC-072 — Creature symbols restart at A within each group

**Closed 2026-08-17** — answered by the user at this batch's gate (option (a) of two).

- **Question.** IN-064 asks for token symbols that "read A-Z" — three goblins labelled A, B,
  C. When a second batch is added, do the letters continue (D, E, F) or restart at A?
- **Answer.** **Restart at A, scoped to the group.** Three Goblins in one group are A, B, C;
  three Orcs in their own group are A, B, C again.
- **Why.** It is what the request describes literally, and the letter is read *next to* the
  name — "Goblin 2 / B" — with the group card and the encounter board both already showing
  which group a card belongs to. The letter's job is to tell one goblin from another goblin,
  which is a within-group question.
- **What is given up.** Two tokens on the same map can both read "A". The scheme being
  replaced avoided that by spending the letter on the creature *type* instead
  (`nextCreatureTypeLetter` → `a1`, `a2`, `a3` for the first type, `b1`… for the second), so
  the letter was unique room-wide and the *number* distinguished within a batch. That
  uniqueness is the cost of the change, and it is deliberate: the alternative continues into
  D, E, F, which stops matching "goblin 1, goblin 2, goblin 3" and runs out at 26 creatures
  per room.
- **Impact.** `defaultCreatureRefs` and `nextCreatureTypeLetter`
  (`apps/web/src/lib/tokens/labels.ts`) are rewritten against the group rather than against
  every unowned token in the room, and `labels.test.ts` moves with them. Seat letters
  (`seatLetterFor`, players A, B, C by join order) are a separate scheme and are
  **untouched** — they were already uppercase and already per-room.

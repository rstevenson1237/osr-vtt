## DEC-112 — A `.dd2vtt`/`.uvtt` import makes a new map of walls and doors, and nothing else

**Closed 2026-09-23** — answered by the user under `/work-item` (IN-186).

- **Question.** Where does an imported Universal VTT file land, and what happens to the parts
  the app cannot hold (the embedded image, lights)?
- **Answer.** (user, 2026-09-23) **A new square-grid map** sized from `resolution.map_size`,
  holding the file's `line_of_sight` and `objects_line_of_sight` as walls with
  `source: 'imported'`, and its `portals` as doors. **The embedded image and lights are ignored
  silently.** Nothing existing is overwritten.
- **Why the conversion is RULE-006-clean.** UVTT coordinates are already grid units; lattice
  units are `point − map_origin`, floats, never pixels (`pixels_per_grid` is discarded). A
  portal's `bounds[0]`/`bounds[1]` are the door's `a`/`b`; `closed` gives its state. No schema
  change — `imported` is already a `WallSource`.
- **Alternatives.** Importing into the current map, or offering both; prompting for a
  background URL after import; keeping the image in the local build's `.vttcamp`.
